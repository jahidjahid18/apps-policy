Kids Monitor account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your Kids Monitor parent account and its associated data by emailing support@kidsmonitor.com.

Use the subject line exactly as shown below and send the request from the email address associated with your Kids Monitor account:

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Email Account Deletion Request

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Parent account profile
  • Child profiles
  • Location history
  • Safe Areas

Other account-linked safety data is also removed where applicable, including pairing information, alerts, and parent access links.

Records That May Be Retained

Financial and subscription records may be retained as required by Google Play, RevenueCat, applicable tax rules, fraud-prevention requirements, or other legal obligations. These records are not used for monitoring or advertising.

When another parent or guardian remains linked to a child, shared child data may remain available to that remaining guardian. Your own account access and guardian link will be removed.

Processing

We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request. Deletion requests are handled within the period required by applicable law and Google Play policy.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Kids Monitor processes and retains data, read the Kids Monitor Privacy Policy.
