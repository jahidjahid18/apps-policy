# Kids Monitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Kids Monitor ("we", "our", "the app") is a family safety application with parent and child companion apps.

## Who Can Use the App

- Parent or legal guardian accounts are responsible for child device setup.
- Child device monitoring must only be used with required legal consent under local laws.

## Information We Process

- Account information (such as email and profile details) for sign-in and account management.
- Device and pairing data needed to securely link parent and child devices.
- Location data (live location, last location, movement history, safe area status).
- Safety alerts and events (SOS alerts, geofence alerts, permission health, app-block events).
- On paired child devices: the list of installed apps and app usage or foreground-app events used for app blocking.
- Voice messages, only if one is sent through a linked device.
- Optional profile image selected by the user.
- Purchase and subscription status managed through Google Play and our subscription provider.
- Technical logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

## Permissions Used

Depending on feature use, the app may request:

- Location permissions (foreground/background) for live tracking and safety alerts.
- Notifications permission for alerts and status updates.
- Accessibility and usage access permissions for app and website blocking (Android). The accessibility service reads the foreground package name and supported browser address bar only; it does not collect screen text, passwords, or keystrokes.
- Camera permission, used only to scan a pairing QR code.
- Battery optimization exemption so monitoring keeps working when the device is idle.

Permissions are used for safety features and can be changed in device settings, but disabling some permissions may limit app functionality.

On a child device, the app displays an ongoing notification while protection is active and lets the child stop monitoring at any time.

## How We Use Data

- To provide parental safety features, including live map, SOS, and safe areas.
- To deliver alerts when configured conditions are met.
- To maintain subscription-based feature controls and update intervals.
- To protect accounts, prevent misuse, and improve reliability.

## Data Sharing

Kids Monitor does not sell personal data.

Data may be processed by service providers required for app operation, such as:

- Firebase services (authentication, database, storage, messaging, crash reporting).
- Google Maps for map display and location search.
- Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for subscriptions and purchases.

## Advertising

Kids Monitor does not contain advertising SDKs and does not use the Android advertising ID. We do not sell personal data or use child data for advertising.

## Sound Around

The Sound Around control plays an alert sound on the paired child device to help a parent locate it. It does not record, listen to, or transmit ambient audio.

## Data Retention

- Location history is retained for 30 days on paid plans, 3 days during the trial, and 1 day on the free plan.
- Alerts and safety events follow the plan history limit and are then deleted by scheduled retention jobs.
- Account and pairing data are retained while the account is active, or until deletion is requested.
- Parents can delete their account and account-linked data in the app or by using the [account deletion page](kidsmonitor-delete.html).

## Security

- We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data.
- No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; users should secure their devices and accounts.

## Children and Family Safety

- Kids Monitor is designed for family safety use by parents/guardians.
- Parents are responsible for lawful use and proper consent before monitoring a child device.
- The app is not directed to children under 13. Accounts are created and controlled by a parent or legal guardian.
- A child device is paired by a parent, shows an ongoing notification, and can stop monitoring at any time.

## User Controls

- Parents can manage linked child devices, safe areas, and alerts.
- Users can revoke permissions from system settings.
- Users can permanently delete their account from Settings or the [account deletion page](kidsmonitor-delete.html).

## Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is published on this page with the updated date.
