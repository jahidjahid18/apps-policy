Kids Monitor Account Deletion

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Kids Monitor account and the data associated with it. The Android app package is com.kidsmonitor.parent.

Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open Kids Monitor and sign in as the parent or guardian.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm the request.
  4. Complete Google sign-in again if the app asks you to re-authenticate.

Account deletion is permanent. You will be signed out and returned to the welcome screen.

Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the Google account used for Kids Monitor. Use the subject line Kids Monitor Account Deletion.

What Is Deleted

  • Your parent account and profile (name, email, photo, sign-in identifiers).
  • Pairing codes, family links, and parent access audit records.
  • Alerts, SOS records, safe-area definitions, and app-control settings you own.
  • Voice messages and audio files you own, plus related call sessions and inbox entries.
  • Subscription status data stored by the app.
  • Location history and child-device records when no other parent or guardian remains linked.

What May Be Retained

  • If another parent or guardian is still linked to a child device, that child's data remains available to them. Your access and parent link are removed.
  • Limited records may be retained where required by law, fraud prevention, security, or dispute resolution. Payment and purchase records are handled by Google Play and the subscription provider under their own retention rules.
  • Encrypted backup copies may remain for up to 30 days before being overwritten or deleted through the normal backup cycle.

Processing Time

In-app deletion starts immediately. Email requests are verified and processed within 30 days. We may contact you if we need to confirm account ownership.

Remove One Child Device

To remove monitoring for one child without deleting your parent account, open the child's profile in the app and use the remove or delete child option. This removes the linked child data from your account.
